# Getting Started

## Site Settings
- Please navigate to `/data/site_settings.json` and put a name on your site.
  - you could even just navigate to `/admin` and create your first account then go to `settings` and setup your site.

## All of your content should be stored in:
`/public`
  - `/pages` is where your pages are stored (Like this file)
  - `/modules` is where your heavy lifting modules reside, need idea's, see the `example_module`
  - `/plugins` is where you extend the core without touching the core, please see the `example_plugin`
  - `/themes` is where your visual appearance is stored, want to create your own? start with the `example_theme`
  
## We ask that you
**DO NOT** mess with the core, as it could damage your site. We extend into `modules`, `pages`, `plugins` and `themes` so that there is no need to tamper with the core.

## Everything
Can be managed in the Sites `/admin` platform which you will have access too once you establish the first admin account, and a little note here, once you do so, Registrations will be automatically shut off. You can enable them again if you wish, in `/admin?action=settings`
